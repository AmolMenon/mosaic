# User Guide

Welcome to Mosaic OS. This guide will help you understand the operational loops and workflows of our platform.

## The Core Loop

1. **Create a Workspace**: Organize your diligence around a specific deal or company.
2. **Upload Documents**: Drag and drop Confidential Information Memorandums (CIMs), financial models, or expert network transcripts into the workspace.
3. **Run AI Execution**: Trigger the pipeline. Mosaic OS will extract entities, synthesize evidence, and formulate hypotheses using adversarial debate (Claude vs. GPT-4o).
4. **Review IC Memo**: Once the pipeline completes, download or view the generated Investment Committee (IC) Memo, complete with line-by-line citations mapping back to the source documents.

## Managing False Positives
Mosaic OS is designed to flag contradictions in data (e.g., Q3 revenue stated as $10M in the deck, but $8M in the model). Use the **Telemetry Dashboard** to investigate the exact LLM reasoning steps that led to an alert.
