# Pricing Strategy

Mosaic OS operates on a B2B SaaS usage-based tier model, designed to capture value from boutique investment teams up to Tier-1 enterprise funds.

## Free Tier
- **Target**: Evaluators, analysts testing the tool.
- **Price**: $0
- **Limits**: 1 Active Project, 100 pages of parsed data total, Standard LLMs (GPT-4o-mini).
- **Rationale**: Lowers the barrier to entry, allowing analysts to experience the "magic moment" of automated extraction without requiring immediate procurement approval.

## Pro Tier
- **Target**: Boutique Private Equity, Strategy Consulting teams.
- **Price**: $499/user/month (Billed annually)
- **Limits**: Unlimited Projects, Unlimited Data Ingestion, Premium LLMs (Claude 3.5 Sonnet, GPT-4o).
- **Rationale**: Captures high-frequency users who require rigorous reasoning capabilities and deal velocity.

## Enterprise Tier
- **Target**: Tier-1 Funds, Global Investment Banks.
- **Price**: Custom (Starting at $50k/year)
- **Limits**: Unlimited everything + Custom Features.
- **Features**: Dedicated VPC Peering, SOC2 Compliance Reports, Custom Fine-tuned Heuristics, White-glove Onboarding.
- **Rationale**: Addresses strict regulatory, data residency, and InfoSec requirements mandated by large institutions.
