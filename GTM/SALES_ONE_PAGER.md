# Mosaic OS — The AI Operating System for Investment Intelligence

## The Problem
Private Equity and Corporate Strategy teams spend 80% of their time on data extraction and only 20% on analysis. The modern data room is a chaotic repository of unstructured data: 500-page CIMs, complex financial models, and dozens of expert network transcripts. Traditional horizontal LLM tools (like ChatGPT or Claude) cannot synthesize this volume of data reliably without hallucinating or losing context.

## The Solution
Mosaic OS is a specialized orchestration engine that automates thesis extraction, evidence synthesis, and adversarial diligence.

## Key Differentiators
1. **Adversarial Orchestration**: Mosaic OS doesn't just ask a single LLM a question. It uses a multi-agent architecture to pit models against each other. Claude 3.5 Sonnet extracts evidence, and GPT-4o audits the extraction for contradictions, ensuring maximum fidelity.
2. **Infinite Context Window**: Through intelligent chunking, vector embedding, and recursive summarization, Mosaic OS can ingest a data room of virtually unlimited size.
3. **Traceable Citations**: Every claim generated in the final IC Memo is linked via a traceable citation back to the exact page, paragraph, and line in the source document.
4. **Enterprise Security**: SOC2 Type II architecture. Row-level tenant isolation. Zero model training on customer data.

## ROI & Impact
- **Time Savings**: Reduce the time required to generate a preliminary screening memo from 2 weeks to 15 minutes.
- **Deeper Diligence**: Analyze 100% of the data room, rather than relying on sampling or keyword searches.
- **Deal Velocity**: Move faster on high-conviction targets, leaving competitors behind in the reading phase.
